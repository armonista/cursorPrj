Dusk delivery

Clockwork Courier

Carry the parcel across the brass rooftops and drop it in the clock-tower slot.

How to play

  • Move left and right with the arrow keys, or A and D.
  • Jump with Space, the Up arrow, or W.
  • Hold jump a little longer for a slightly higher jump.

Pocket the brass springs. That tally is your score. Chimneys puff before the steam hits, and a slow gear turns in one gap. Touching either costs a life. You have three lives. A checkpoint on the middle roofs saves your place.
